Format 2:1 (np. 1200×600 px) — kontener na stronie ma `aspect-[2/1]`.

**Alt:** Kandydat pracujący nad CV z pomocą narzędzia AI

**Prompt:**
A realistic wide photo of a person at a desk with a laptop open beside an open paper notebook filled with handwritten notes, one hand resting on the notebook as if cross-checking, soft natural daylight, shallow depth of field, warm professional tones, screen content blurred and unreadable, no visible readable text or logos, careful and deliberate mood, shot in 2:1 landscape framing
